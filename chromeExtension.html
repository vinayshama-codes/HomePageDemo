Astrea Smart vCard For Salesforce

With Astrea Smart vCard Chrome Extension users can manage their contacts efficiently. Users can transfer contacts from Salesforce to their phone via email. It allows exporting Salesforce contacts from Accounts, Contacts, and Leads as VCF 3.0 files.

Astrea Listview Export for Salesforce

Astrea Excel Generator is a Google Chrome extension for Salesforce that downloads Listview contents in Classic. It exports large datasets from both standard and custom objects to Excel format with all required fields.

Learn More Chrome Store

Astrea Salesforce Activities

With Astrea My Activities Chrome extension, users can access tasks and events for the current date or the next three days. It shows upcoming events, pending overdue tasks, and hides completed tasks and events for efficient tracking.

Learn More Chrome Store

Astrea Share Salesforce Records

Astrea Share Salesforce Records is a Chrome extension for Salesforce Lightning that allows sharing of records, including custom objects and standard objects like Account, Contact, and Lead, a functionality absent in Lightning by default.
